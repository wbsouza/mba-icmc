\chapter{Appendix(es)}
Optional element consisting of a text or document elaborated by the author to complement their argument, as per ABNT NBR 14724.

Appendices must be identified by consecutive uppercase letters, followed by a hyphen and their titles. Exceptionally, doubled uppercase letters are used when the 26 letters of the alphabet are exhausted. Pagination must be continuous with the main text.

\chapter{Example of a vertically and horizontally centered table}
\index{tables}\autoref{tab-centralizada} illustrates how to obtain a table that is centered vertically and horizontally.
\begin{table}[htb]
\ABNTEXfontereduzida
\caption[Example of a vertically and horizontally centered table]{Example of a vertically and horizontally centered table}
\label{tab-centralizada}

\begin{tabular}{ >{\centering\arraybackslash}m{6cm}  >{\centering\arraybackslash}m{6cm} }
\hline
 \centering \textbf{Column A} & \textbf{Column B}\\
\hline
  Column A, row 1 & This is a much longer text used to demonstrate how content is centered vertically and horizontally in the table. Second paragraph to confirm the table layout.\\
  When the text in column A, row 2 is much longer than in the other columns & Column B, row 2\\
\hline
\end{tabular}
\begin{flushleft}
		Source: prepared by the authors.\
\end{flushleft}
\end{table}
